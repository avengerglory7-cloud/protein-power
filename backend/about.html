About Protein Power 💪

Welcome to Protein Power — your companion for smarter nutrition and a healthier fitness journey.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make nutrition information easier to understand and everyday fitness tracking more accessible. We want to help people make more informed food choices and stay motivated towards their fitness goals.

What Protein Power Offers

🤖 AI Food Scanner

Upload a photo of your food and our AI-powered scanner estimates its calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugar and fiber.

💪 Protein Calculator

Estimate your daily protein needs based on the information you provide.

📊 Nutrition Dashboard

Review your saved food scans and see estimated nutrition totals for your day.

🥗 Protein-Rich Food Guides

Explore different food options, including vegetarian foods, non-vegetarian foods, supplements and snacks.

Our Approach

We believe that understanding what you eat can help you make better everyday decisions. Protein Power brings useful nutrition tools together in one convenient place.

Important Health Disclaimer

Protein Power provides general nutrition estimates and educational information. AI-generated food analysis may not always be accurate. Our tools are not a substitute for professional medical, dietary or nutritional advice.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian for advice tailored to your needs.
