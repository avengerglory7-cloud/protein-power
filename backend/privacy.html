Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to Protein Power. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit and use our website and AI food nutrition scanner.

1. Information We Handle

When you use our AI food scanner, you may upload a photograph of food. The image is processed to estimate nutritional information such as calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugar and fiber.

Our website may also store your food scan history and related nutrition information in your browser's local storage so you can view your previous scans.

2. How Food Images Are Processed

Uploaded food images are temporarily held in server memory while they are processed. Our current backend code does not intentionally save these uploaded images to permanent server storage or a database.

Images are sent to Google's Gemini AI service for food identification and nutritional analysis. Google's handling of submitted data is subject to its applicable terms and privacy policies.

3. Local Storage

Protein Power uses browser local storage to maintain scan history and related information on your device. This information is stored in your browser rather than in a Protein Power user account.

You can clear your browser data or use the website's available history controls to remove locally stored scan history. Clearing browser data may also remove this information.

4. Third-Party Services

Protein Power uses third-party services, including Google Gemini for AI-powered food analysis and Render for website and backend hosting. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their respective policies and terms.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use server-side processing and environment variables to help protect sensitive configuration information, including API credentials.

Uploaded images are handled in server memory by our current application code. However, we cannot guarantee that all third-party providers or hosting infrastructure retain no operational data or logs.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies through its hosting or third-party services. Browser local storage is also used for scan history. You can manage browser storage and cookie settings through your browser.

7. Children's Privacy

Protein Power is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
